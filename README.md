## Project Overview
This project was part of my Master’s program. The goal was to design a go-to-market strategy for launching an electric scooter service in Berlin Mitte, targeting urban commuters and tourists while ensuring operational efficiency.

## Objectives
- Identify key commuter and tourist segments
- Analyze competitors and market potential
- Design pricing, fleet deployment, and marketing strategy
- Simulate launch and forecast outcomes

## Key Insights
- Targeting high-density commuter and tourist areas maximized potential adoption
- Projected **€1.5M first-year revenue**
- Operational improvements could reduce downtime by **20%**
- Achieved **75% peak fleet utilization** in pilot simulation
